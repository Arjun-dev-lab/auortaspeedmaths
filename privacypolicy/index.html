Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 25, 2026

Auorta Speed Maths is developed by ASK Productions. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

Auorta Speed Maths may collect limited non-personal information automatically through third-party services for improving app performance and user experience.

This information may include:

  • App usage statistics
  • Device type and Android version
  • Crash reports and diagnostics
  • Performance and analytics data
  • Network connection status
  • Anonymous identifiers used by third-party services

2. Notifications

Auorta Speed Maths uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send notifications such as:

  • App updates
  • Feature announcements
  • Practice reminders
  • Important service notifications

On supported Android versions, the app requests notification permission before sending notifications. Users can disable notifications anytime from device settings.

3. Internet and Network Access

The app requires internet access to:

  • Load online exam content and study materials
  • Deliver notifications
  • Support analytics and app functionality

The app may also check network availability to ensure proper functionality.

4. Multiplayer Feature

Auorta Speed Maths includes a local two-player mode that allows two users to play on the same device.

This feature does not require account creation and does not collect personal information from players.

5. Personal Information

Auorta Speed Maths does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Full name
  • Home address
  • Passwords
  • Banking or payment information
  • Government identification details

6. Third-Party Services

The app may use trusted third-party services that may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase
  • Meta / Facebook SDK

7. Advertising and Analytics

Third-party SDKs such as Firebase or Meta/Facebook services may use anonymous identifiers and diagnostic information to improve app stability, analytics, and related services.

These services may collect device-related information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We work to protect user information using reasonable security practices. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Auorta Speed Maths is designed for educational purposes. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.
